# Design principles (no code/assets copied)
Awwwards could not be browsed in this pass; direction is based on widely known editorial/Swiss hospitality patterns:
- Oversized wordmark typography with masked reveals
- Asymmetric 12-column grids, thin rules, generous negative space
- Printed-menu style lists with dotted leaders
- One signature motion: the DOT circle expanding (clip-path) into a section
