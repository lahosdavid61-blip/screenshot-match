## Research outcome and publishing policy

The multi-source investigation confirmed the venue identity and the contact data above. The public sources did **not** expose a current, itemized 2026 menu with reliable product-level RSD prices. Restaurant Guru's displayed RSD 1–500 per-person range is an aggregator estimate without item, serving size, date or official provenance, so it is not published as a menu price.

The research observed public image references, including 53 Restaurant Guru photos, 102 Google Maps photos and a 21-photo Subotica.com album from 18 May 2024 credited to Dejan Malagurski. No image was copied into the website because authorship, rights chain and reuse permission were not established. The gallery therefore remains an owner-approval state rather than using stock, generated or unlicensed business photography.
